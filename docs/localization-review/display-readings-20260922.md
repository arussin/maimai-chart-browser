# English pronunciation accessories - September 22, 2026

Reviewed by Codex using an AI linguistic pass over canonical title/artist pairs
and the existing Tachi aliases pinned at
`f08148f8644e40de9b178445df4bd59da712d3de`. This is not native-speaker certification.

The earlier display used only authored kana pronunciations and kana-only titles.
It excluded 474 Japanese-script song records, including **馬と鹿**, even though
445 of those records already had Latin search aliases. The audit reviewed those
445 candidates and selected 321 full-title pronunciations for secondary display.
Translations, nicknames, partial names and uncertain or visibly mistyped readings
remain search-only. Examples excluded: `horse and deer`, `The Snow White Princess
is`, `long song`, and the incomplete `yoake made`. The selected entry for **馬と鹿**
is **Uma to Shika**; **深海少女** uses **Shinkai Shoujo**.

`song-display-readings.json` records every accepted song ID, exact title/artist,
reading, source revision and review method. The compiler rejects identity drift,
unknown aliases, conflicting readings and a changed upstream alias revision.
Capitalization alone may differ from the source. Existing reviewed kana readings
continue to work. Source aliases, canonical titles, artists and catalog identity
are untouched; translations are never automatically promoted into pronunciations.

Loanwords retain established Latin spellings, such as `Girl` or `Shoujo` as
appropriate to the source title. Remaining gaps are explicitly unresolved and
must not be filled from unreviewed Japanese sorting keys, which can omit voicing
or small kana. The original title remains dominant and pronunciation text is
visible only in English. This display review does not certify regional names.
