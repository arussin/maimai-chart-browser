Privacy & analytics

Personal results, attempts and recommendations stay in this tab's memory. They are not uploaded or added to public catalogs. Clearing your results or reloading removes them.

On maimai.party, optional Google Analytics uses cookies to count visits, page views and basic device and country information. It loads only after you allow analytics. We send only the broad Charts, Pattern dictionary, Compare charts and About views. Imported files, player identifiers, search terms, chart selections, full URLs and referrers are excluded. Advertising personalization and Google signals are off.

Your analytics choice is saved on this device for up to 180 days; analytics cookies expire after 90 days. Use Analytics settings below to change your choice. Turning analytics off removes this site's Google Analytics cookies and stops further collection, without clearing your results. Browser privacy signals and local previews disable analytics.

Google processes analytics requests, including your IP address when connecting. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites. Previously collected data is not removed by changing this device's choice.
